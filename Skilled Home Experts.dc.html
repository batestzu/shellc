About S.H.E.

Skilled Home Experts

A woman-owned enterprise bringing a woman's touch and professional results to homes across the Central Savannah River Area.

Owned by mothers, built for comfort

Skilled Home Experts is a Woman Owned Enterprise, locally owned by mothers who understand what it means to want a safe, respectful crew in your home. We serve Augusta, GA, Aiken, SC, and the surrounding CSRA.

We cater specifically to women and seniors who may feel uneasy having men in their homes — and to anyone who simply prefers the comfort of an all-women team. Licensed, insured, and genuinely proud of our work.

Our mission statement

As a woman-owned business we are committed to creating a safe, respectful and welcoming experience for every customer. We proudly serve women, seniors, individuals with disabilities, and anyone who prefers the comfort of an all-woman team. We respect the privacy, cultural traditions, religious beliefs and personal boundaries of every household.
